## Engineering Approach

I treat AI engineering as a production discipline, not just a model-building exercise.

- Start with measurable business and technical outcomes
- Build strong data foundations before adding complexity
- Establish baselines and evaluate quality with the right metrics
- Combine deterministic software engineering with probabilistic AI behavior
- Design for scale, reliability, cost, latency, security, and observability
- Monitor models and AI applications continuously after deployment
- Keep systems maintainable through clean APIs, testing, documentation, and ownership

> Good AI systems create measurable value, earn user trust, and remain dependable as data and requirements change.
